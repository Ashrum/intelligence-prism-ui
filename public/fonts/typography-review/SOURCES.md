# Mathematics font

The current coss UI uses system fonts for text and the complete, unmodified STIX Two Math font for MathML. The former typography comparison and its CJK/NewCM assets have been removed.

- File: `stix-two-math.woff2`
- Family/version: STIX Two Math 2.13 b171
- Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stipub/stixfonts/v2.13b171/fonts/static_otf_woff2/STIXTwoMath-Regular.woff2
- License: `STIX-OFL.txt` (SIL Open Font License)
- SHA-256: `094191335def3f0452c81ec0713cfc2f29bb6af8cecbf79b60881fbf2db97562`
- The complete OpenType MATH tables and glyphs are retained.
